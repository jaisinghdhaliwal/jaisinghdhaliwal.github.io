Overview

Alongside my work at GadJet, I support Vi Cà Phê with product graphics and visual content. The brand is run by one of the managers I work with at GadJet and currently takes orders online while its product range continues to develop.

My contribution covers the physical products and the imagery used to present them. I did not design or develop the Vi Cà Phê website.

Designing the products

The cup and can artwork is created in Adobe Illustrator, adapting the Vi Cà Phê identity across different proportions while keeping the products recognisable as one range.

Visualising the range

Before the canned drinks had been produced, I used Blender to explore how a possible range could look. The drinks were intentionally speculative at this stage, using AI-generated textures to represent different flavours inside preliminary can models.

Before there were physical products, 3D mockups helped make the range tangible.

From mockup to product

The cans have since been manufactured and Vi Cà Phê is taking online orders. Having the real product now provides a more reliable reference for photography and for updating the 3D asset so future renders better reflect the manufactured can.

In use

I also create product imagery and selected social media graphics for Vi Cà Phê. The finished assets support the online presentation of the drinks while keeping the physical product design as the visual anchor.
