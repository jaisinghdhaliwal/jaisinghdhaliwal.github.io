Overview

Vi Cà Phê is a new Vietnamese coffee brand currently selling online. I support the brand across packaging, product graphics, 3D visualisation and imagery as its product range develops.

My work focuses on the physical products and the visual assets used to present them. Website design and development are handled by others.

Designing the products

The cup and can artwork is created in Adobe Illustrator, adapting the Vi Cà Phê identity across different proportions while keeping the products recognisable as one range. The colour system uses specified Pantone colours, including PANTONE 7599 C and PANTONE P 23-1, helping maintain consistency across the printed packaging.

Visualising the range

Before the canned drinks had been manufactured, I used Blender to explore how a possible range could look. At this stage the products were deliberately speculative, with preliminary can models and AI-generated drink textures used to quickly visualise different flavour and presentation possibilities.

Before there were physical products, 3D mockups helped make the range tangible.

From mockup to product

The cans have since been manufactured and Vi Cà Phê is taking online orders. With the finished product now available as a physical reference, I can compare its proportions, artwork placement and construction against the original preliminary model and update the 3D asset to more accurately reflect the manufactured can.

  1. Concept model
  2. Production
  3. Physical reference
  4. Accurate digital model

The process has effectively reversed: the original 3D model helped visualise the product before manufacture, while the manufactured product now provides the reference for a more accurate digital version.

In use

I continue to create product imagery and selected social media graphics for Vi Cà Phê as the brand develops. The finished assets support online product presentation and launch communications while keeping the physical packaging as the main visual anchor.

The project has developed from speculative product visualisation into a manufactured product range, connecting packaging design, physical production, 3D modelling and commercial imagery within one ongoing workflow.
