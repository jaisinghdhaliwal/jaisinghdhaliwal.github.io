Design/Foundations/Logo

One mark. Four useful formats.

J/SD is the compact signature of Jai Singh Dhaliwal. The current system uses one finished mark in landscape and square formats, plus full-name lockups for light and dark surfaces.

01 / The system

A compact mark and a clear signature.

The coral holding shape and custom navy J/SD lettering form the core identity. Use the compact mark when Jai's name is already visible or space is limited. Use the full-name lockup when the identity needs to introduce itself.

01

Keep the artwork fixed

Do not retype the letters, redraw the slash, change the spacing or alter the proportions.

02

Choose by format

Landscape suits navigation and horizontal layouts. Square suits avatars, icons and compact applications.

03

Use one signature

Show the logo once in a composition so it identifies the work without competing with it.

03 / Formats

Use the version made for the available space.

Landscape mark

The default for website navigation, small print signatures and horizontal interface spaces.

Square mark

For app icons, social avatars and genuinely square placements.

Navy name lockup

For light surfaces, formal documents and introductions where the full name adds useful context.

White name lockup

For the navy footer and other controlled dark surfaces.

04 / Space and scale

Give the fixed artwork room to read.

Keep a clear area around the coral field and scale the supplied file as one object. Do not crop into the holding shape or enlarge the lettering independently.

Landscape markUse at its established 2:1 proportion. Test legibility at the final rendered or printed size.
Square markReserve for square applications rather than forcing the landscape mark into a small icon.
Full-name lockupUse only where the full name has room to remain clear and comfortable.
AlignmentAlign the complete asset to a strong edge or grid line without modifying the SVG.

05 / Usage

Consistency is the distinctive feature.

Do

Use the supplied SVG

It keeps the custom construction and exact colour relationship intact at every size.

Avoid

Rebuilding it with a font

The logo is not ordinary J/SD text set in Poppins or Fira Code.

Do

Use the white lockup on navy

This is the established footer treatment and provides a clear formal signature.

Avoid

Inventing extra variants

Do not recolour, reshape or add a new holding device to the approved logo files.
