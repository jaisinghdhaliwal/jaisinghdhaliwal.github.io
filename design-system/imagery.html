Imagery

The work supplies the colour, texture and surprise. The interface gives every image enough structure and space to be understood.

On this page Principles Project imagery Showreel Photography 3D & scenes Delivery

01 / Principles

Let the work carry the colour.

Portfolio media should feel vivid and varied, while its framing remains calm. Avoid applying a single colour treatment across every discipline; a photograph, identity project and rendered scene should retain their own character.

01

Show the result clearly

Choose a frame that communicates the project before it demonstrates a visual effect. The strongest image should not need explanatory overlay copy.

02

Use depth at the edge

Frames, shadows and glass captions may give an image physical presence, but they must not obscure important detail.

03

Respect the source

Photography keeps its native aspect ratio, 3D objects keep clean silhouettes, and Blender environments retain a panoramic sense of space.

04

Never depend on hover

Titles and essential context stay visible on touch devices. Hover may add scale and elevation, never missing information.

03 / Showreel

Begin with movement, not controls.

The showreel is an atmospheric introduction rather than another project card. It sits behind the hero copy, plays muted and inline, and yields gracefully to a still image when motion or data should be reduced.

Desktop

Use a left-to-right paper gradient so primary-dark copy remains legible while the right side of the video stays visible.

Mobile

Use a near-square crop and a shorter vertical fade. Allow the heading to overlap the transition slightly so image and copy feel connected.

PlaybackMuted, inline and without visible controls. The combined showreel is supplied as H.264 and H.265 MP4 files.
FallbackUse matching AVIF and WebP poster images taken from the first frame so the transition into video is not visible.
User settingsShow the poster for prefers-reduced-motion and data-saving users.
Copy focusAfter inactivity, the full introduction may reduce to a small glass label. Any pointer activity restores it.

04 / Photography

Preserve the shape of the photograph.

Travel photography is presented as a continuously moving horizontal sequence. Landscape, portrait and square images may sit together at one common height; never force every photograph into 3:2.

Physical-photo treatment

Use an 8-pixel white frame and a soft primary-dark shadow. Keep images aligned in one horizontal rail rather than placing them diagonally like a scrapbook.

Do

Write literal alt text, retain original framing and let location diagrams provide supporting context outside the photographs.

Avoid

Heavy filters, artificial HDR, diagonal piles, fixed aspect-ratio classes and captions that cover the image.

05 / 3D & scenes

Show form, space and process differently.

Use the presentation method that best explains the work instead of repeating the photography carousel for every medium.

Scanned objectsUse isolated object cutouts on paper or coral-soft. A carousel may switch models; AR is a separate action.
Blender scenesUse a wide panoramic render with a short title and description to its left on desktop.
ProcessUse one timed image window to move between Workbench, Blender and Fortnite views, or a swipe comparison when the visitor needs direct control.
Reflective workUse real rendering, lighting and material studies to express the brand’s interest in light; avoid unrelated decorative 3D shapes.

06 / Delivery

Prepare the right image before adding effects.

Project thumbnailsthumb-720.avif inside each project folder, matched to the first video frame.
PhotographyUse compact WebP images in the scrolling carousel and link each one to its larger AVIF version in the image viewer.
Detail imageryUse descriptive names and responsive widths. Avoid placing important text near crop boundaries.
LoadingLoad the hero poster eagerly; lazy-load off-screen project and archive images.
ColourExport consistently and test HDR separately. Do not let selected bright assets make the rest of the paper interface appear unintentionally grey.
Alternative textDescribe the visible subject or result, not the file type and not “image of.” Decorative duplicates use empty alt text.
