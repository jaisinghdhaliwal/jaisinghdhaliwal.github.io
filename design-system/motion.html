Design/Expression/Motion

Motion

Movement should reveal hierarchy, connect states and make spatial relationships easier to understand. It should never delay access to the work.

On this page Principles Timing Easing Patterns Interaction Reduced motion

01 / Principles

Move to explain.

The identity uses motion to show where something came from, what became active and which layer is in focus. Every animation needs a functional reason.

01

Preserve continuity

Shared elements such as the active navigation pill should travel between states while their labels remain stable.

02

Reveal in context

Project cards enter individually when they reach the viewport, not all at once when the page loads.

03

Reward, do not obstruct

Hover can add depth and scale, but no control or information may appear only after hover.

04

Respect attention

Autoplaying media is muted and calm. Continuous motion pauses when the page is hidden, focused for interaction or reduced motion is requested.

02 / Timing

A short, deliberate scale.

Use the quickest duration that communicates the change clearly. Similar actions should feel related across pages.

Motion demonstration
Fast / 180msButton hover, colour response and small control feedback.
Standard / 280–300msCard elevation, navigation growth on fine pointers and component state changes.
Page / 420msActive navigation pill travel and cross-page entrance.
Media / 500msShowreel clip crossfade and image transitions.
Reveal / 620msProject card entrance when it reaches the viewport.
Expressive / 700msHero focus-mode reduction and other intentional spatial transitions.

03 / Easing

Responsive at the start, calm at the end.

Use easing that leaves quickly and settles smoothly. Avoid elastic or bouncy behaviour; it conflicts with the precise, minimal tone.

Standard spatialcubic-bezier(.2, .75, .25, 1) for project cards, reveals and focus transitions.
Shared elementcubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1) for navigation, page entrance and interface layers.
Simple fadeease where opacity is the only property changing.
AvoidLinear UI movement, spring overshoot, bounce and long decorative loops.

04 / Patterns

Recognisable motion behaviours.

Navigation continuity

WorkCreateCV

The coral pill moves to the destination. All three labels remain visible and stationary throughout the page transition.

Project entrance

Opacity 0 → 1

Translate Y 28px → 0

Scale .98 → 1

Trigger each card once at roughly 12% viewport intersection.

Project focus

Lift the complete card by 7px, scale to 1.025 and strengthen its shadow over 280ms. Scale the image internally to 1.035 over 450ms.

Hero focus mode

After four seconds of inactivity on desktop, reduce the full copy and retain a compact glass label. Scrolling, pointer movement or keyboard activity restores the introduction.

Showreel playback

Play the combined showreel muted and inline, using matching H.264 and H.265 sources with a first-frame poster underneath.

Continuous photography

Scroll steadily at a low speed, duplicate the sequence for a seamless loop and pause during direct manipulation.

05 / Interaction

Adapt motion to the input.

Fine pointerAllow hover elevation, subtle image scale and the desktop navigation expansion.
TouchDo not grow the bottom navigation or require hover to expose content.
KeyboardGive focused interactive cards the same hierarchy as hover and keep a visible focus ring.
DraggingPause automatic rails while the user drags, then resume after a short delay.
Page visibilityPause continuous movement when the document is hidden.
Do

Animate transform and opacity wherever possible so movement stays smooth and does not reflow surrounding content.

Avoid

Animating width, height or layout position when a transform can communicate the same spatial change.

06 / Reduced motion

The experience must remain complete without animation.

When prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is active, smooth scrolling stops, transitions become effectively immediate, project cards remain visible and the showreel uses its poster image.

NavigationMove directly to the selected state without a travelling pill animation.
ProjectsRender fully visible with no entrance translation or scale.
ShowreelDo not autoplay; retain the responsive poster as the complete visual.
CarouselsDisable automatic movement while preserving manual scrolling and controls.
ContentNever hide essential copy behind an animation or timed state.
