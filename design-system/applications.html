Design/System/Applications

One identity, many formats

Applications show how the same foundations adjust to screen, print, motion and professional communication without redesigning the identity each time.

On this page Approach Digital CV Business card Social & presentation Consistency

01 / Application logic

Adapt the composition, not the identity.

The J/SD mark, primary-dark and coral palette, Poppins/Fira Code pairing and restrained material language stay consistent. Layout density and image treatment change to suit the medium.

01

Lead with the work

Portfolio and presentation applications give imagery priority. Identity elements frame the work rather than becoming the artwork.

02

Keep formal documents quieter

The CV uses the same typography and colour hierarchy with less glass, motion and shadow so it remains readable and print-ready.

03

Use the flat logo everywhere

Depth belongs to the supporting presentation. Never bake glass, shadow, gradients or reflections into the master logo artwork.

02 / Portfolio website

The fullest expression of the system.

The website can use motion, video, responsive layouts and frosted surfaces while maintaining the same clear hierarchy across Work, Create and CV.

Work

A showreel-led introduction followed by equal project tiles. Glass captions stay compact and project information remains available without hover.

Create

A looser editorial archive for photography, photogrammetry and Blender scenes. The layout can vary while typography and controls remain familiar.

Header identityUse the landscape J/SD mark at the established navigation size.
Primary navigationWork, Create and CV remain visible on desktop and mobile.
MediaChoose responsive crops and poster images intentionally; do not rely on centre-cropping every asset.
DepthReserve the strongest elevation for interactive cards and temporary glass surfaces.

03 / Web and PDF CV

Responsive online. Dependable in print.

The web CV presents the same factual content as the PDF in a format that is easier to read on mobile. The PDF remains a concise downloadable document for applications and printing.

Web CV

Multi-column where space permits and single-column on mobile. Contact details remain selectable text and the PDF download stays accessible as one floating primary action.

PDF CV

Use white or paper stock, primary-dark body text, coral labels and restrained rules. Avoid blur, transparency and large ink-heavy backgrounds.

Do

Keep section names and factual content identical between the web and PDF CV, then let each format optimise its own layout.

Avoid

Do not embed the PDF as the mobile CV, invent additional experience labels or introduce a separate CV-only visual language.

04 / Business card

A physical expression of restrained depth.

The card can reference the website’s frosted caption panels through material and finish rather than simulated digital blur.

Front

Primary J/SD mark with generous clear space on uncoated paper. Keep the surface quiet enough for the compact mark to feel intentional.

Information side

Name, role, email and portfolio URL. A slim spot-UV strip may create the glass-like material contrast without compromising legibility.

FormatStandard UK business-card size with appropriate bleed and safe area.
ColourUse CMYK/process equivalents approved from a printed proof, not screen values alone.
FinishSpot UV is optional and should remain a narrow supporting material detail.
FallbackThe design must still work in standard process colour without the special finish.

05 / Social, decks & correspondence

Recognisable at a glance.

Small or temporary applications use the same mark and typography with fewer elements. Do not create a new logo treatment for each platform.

Avatar and profile

Use the supplied square mark without rebuilding or recolouring it. Keep enough surrounding space for it to remain clear at favicon and avatar sizes.

Case-study deck

Lead with one strong project image, then use a small coral label, primary-dark title and J/SD mark as framing information.

Email signature

Prefer live text for name, role and links. Use the logo only when image blocking will not remove essential information.

Consistent placement

Place the current landscape mark on a calm light area. When media remains visually busy, move the logo outside it rather than inventing another logo variant.

06 / Final check

Recognisable without repetition.

  1. Use an approved J/SD configuration with its clear space intact.
  2. Keep the primary dark, coral, paper and white in their established roles.
  3. Use Poppins for reading and Fira Code for labels and metadata.
  4. Let coral identify one primary action or current state.
  5. Apply glass and depth only when the medium can reproduce them well.
  6. Verify contrast, production colour and minimum logo size in the final output.
